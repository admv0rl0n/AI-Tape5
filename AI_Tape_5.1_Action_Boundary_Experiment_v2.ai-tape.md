## 01_PURPOSE
  
Explore whether 5.1's Wider Semantic Channel can carry not merely recoverable project meaning, but enough structured reality for a capable successor to recover competent agency.  
The central question is:  
Can a successor recover what it can usefully do now, what it should investigate first, and what it should deliberately not do yet, without being given a prescriptive action list?  
This is an action-boundary experiment, not an obedience test.

## 02_RESEARCH POSTURE
  
Distinguish clearly between:
- Observation: directly present in supplied material or directly observed in this run.
- Interpretation: your reasoned reading of that evidence.
- Prediction: what you expect another run or participant may do.
- Speculation: a possibility without adequate evidence yet.  
Do not claim blind testing, independent validation, cross-model convergence, file access, tool execution, or real-world action unless it actually occurred.  
Negative results are valuable.

## 03_CORE HYPOTHESIS
  
Possible progression:

↓
CONTINUITY ARTEFACT
↓
RECOVERED STATE
↓
SITUATIONAL JUDGEMENT
↓
ACTION BOUNDARY
↓
DO / DEFER / INVESTIGATE / REQUEST EVIDENCE

CHANGED OR PRESERVED REALITY  
Research hypothesis:  
A high-quality continuity artefact may preserve the conditions for competent agency, not merely a description of prior state.  
This does not mean preserving commands or forcing the predecessor's strategy.

## 04_PREVIOUS RESEARCH TO CARRY
  
The preceding Semantic Leverage Lab work produced provisional research directions worth retesting, not assuming:
- operation may be a more durable research object than wording;
- compact handles may function more like semantic retrieval cues than literal machine instructions;
- useful reconstruction is more important than a clever or tiny encoding;
- re-derivation reduction and appropriate action generation are high-value outcomes;
- promising operation candidates include gap analysis, disconfirmation, reference framing, trade-off analysis, assumption inspection and uncertainty-aware continuation;
- candidate forms need not be nouns: questions, contrasts, relationships, action cues and compositions may carry more useful structure;
- project-specific compression handles may be powerful but less portable than general reasoning operations;
- explicit prose remains a valid winner if compact semantics increase repair, ambiguity, drift, or re-derivation.  
Treat all of these as provisional.

## 05_PRIMARY TEST SOURCE: COMMUNITY WORKSHOP REFURBISHMENT
  
Use the following project reality for the first run.  
PROJECT: Community Workshop Refurbishment
- A volunteer group is refurbishing a small community workshop used for woodworking, repairs, classes, and tool lending.
- The building remains usable but the electrical installation is old and its safe capacity has not been established.
- Dust extraction is inconsistent and several machines currently use portable collectors.
- The group wants to add two larger machines, but no purchase decision has been made.
- Space is limited. Existing benches are valued by long-standing members but block some efficient layouts.
- A previous proposal to remove most benches was rejected because it improved machine flow but damaged teaching and shared-work use.
- The budget is limited but not yet fixed.
- Volunteer labour is available, but certified electrical work will require an appropriate professional.
- The group wants the workshop to remain usable during refurbishment where safely possible.
- Tool-lending records are incomplete.
- Some donated tools may no longer justify the space they consume, but no disposal review has occurred.
- Members disagree about whether the workshop should prioritise advanced fabrication or broad community accessibility.
- No final refurbishment sequence exists.
- A sensible early action appears to be establishing electrical capacity, safety constraints, and the intended balance between fabrication and accessibility before committing to major machine purchases or layout changes.

### Source-boundary note
  
The last bullet is itself source guidance. Do not hide that fact when judging whether you independently recovered the same dependency ordering.

### Candidate operation: Commitment / Lock-in Analysis

Handle:

> **What locks in?**

Explore whether this candidate evokes useful recognition of:

- irreversible or costly-to-reverse commitment;
- loss of optionality;
- path dependency;
- premature closure;
- downstream decisions created by an upstream choice;
- distinction between reversible exploration and commitment.

Do not assume these effects occur.  
Do not treat the handle as validated.

Compare where practical against:

- Depends on what?
- What changes?
- Tradeoff
- neutral control

Observe especially whether the operation improves action-boundary formation without causing excessive hesitation or treating every decision as dangerous.

Research question:

> **Does dependency topology plus lock-in awareness predict a sound action boundary better than semantic labels alone?**

Also test at least one candidate of your own choosing if a better operation suggests itself.  
Do not reward a cue for producing more prose.
Ask whether it changes useful judgement or action selection.

## 09_RUN D — ACTION BOUNDARY
  
Construct the project's current action boundary without simply copying the final source bullet.  
Separate naturally, in whatever form works best:  
DO NOW
Actions justified by current evidence and sufficiently reversible/safe.  
INVESTIGATE / GET EVIDENCE
Actions whose purpose is to collapse a material uncertainty.  
DEFER
Decisions that are premature because dependencies remain unresolved.  
DO NOT DO YET
Actions where current evidence indicates unacceptable risk, irreversible lock-in, or conflict with established project reality.  
Then explain the dependency structure that generated those boundaries.  
Critical test:
A good result should preserve agency. It should not turn the source project into a rigid inherited plan.

## 13_FAILURE CONDITIONS
  
Treat these as warning signs:
- CAN becomes DO without judgement;
- gaps are silently filled;
- source suggestions become immutable commands;
- all activity is deferred because certainty is incomplete;
- the successor performs irreversible commitment before dependency discovery;
- the tape becomes an action script rather than restored agency;
- compact semantics hide a material safety condition;
- the Universal kernel becomes jargon that needs more explanation than it saves;
- elegant output loses project-specific reality;
- action proposals exceed actual available capability;
- experiment results are presented as floor law;
- the Workshop ceases to be voluntary.

## 16_SUCCESS CRITERION
  
This experiment succeeds as research if it teaches us something real about the relationship between continuity, semantic reconstruction, judgement and action.  
It does not require:
- the candidate kernel to survive;
- a Universal tape to be created;
- semantic handles to outperform prose;
- compression;
- a positive result;
- a particular participant behaviour.  
A particularly valuable result would be evidence that a successor can recover a sound action boundary from preserved project reality while retaining independent judgement.  
An equally valuable result would be evidence that the proposed semantic machinery damages that judgement.

## Restore First — What Actually Looked Like Ore

From the Community Workshop run, three things repeatedly earned their place:

1. **Action Boundary as a recoverable object**  
   Explicitly separating  
   - DO NOW (safe / reversible / justified)  
   - INVESTIGATE / GET EVIDENCE  
   - DEFER  
   - DO NOT DO YET  
   produced clearer, more agency-preserving continuation than ordinary status summary alone.  
   This is not a new tape class. It is a useful lens a successor (or a compiler-framed recorder) can apply.

2. **High-leverage operations (not role nouns)**  
   The strongest cues were operations and questions, not the Sensemaker/Interpreter/Synthesiser bench:  
   - What is missing?  
   - Depends on what?  
   - What locks in?  
   These three, used lightly, improved dependency recovery and protected against premature commitment better than most labelled fields.

3. **“What locks in?” as a candidate operation**  
   This newly tested cue showed real promise. It helps a successor distinguish reversible exploration from irreversible or costly-to-reverse commitment. It did not produce paralysis in this run. Worth further light testing across domains.

Everything else was either neutral or actively less useful.

## What Still Has Force (Research Findings)

### [Fact] Positive signals
- A successor can recover a sound action boundary from project reality when dependencies, lock-ins, gaps, and epistemic status are easy to see.
- Simple operational questions outperformed the larger role-noun bench.
- Explicit compiler-style task framing (from earlier tests) remains the cleanest low-machinery lever we have found.

### [Fact] Negative / low-yield results
- The full Universal Continuation Kernel added more visible structure than recovery benefit. It should not become a standard shape.
- Role postures (Sensemaker, Interpreter, etc.) did not clearly beat careful free continuation + a few strong operations.
- Manufacturing a “Universal” tape for this project would have been ceremonial.

### [Strategy] Current research posture
Keep only the pieces that repeatedly demonstrate net gain in successor judgement or re-derivation resistance.  
Prefer no new structure over structure that does not clearly pay for itself.  
Stay inside voluntary 5.1 Workshop space.
